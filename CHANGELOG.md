# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-17)

- Settings panel under Options > AddOns (`/agr options`): status, Enabled and verbose toggles, a Pawn scale dropdown per spec, and the ItemRack set list with what follows AutoGear.
- "Pick the sets myself" starts from the sets automatic mode updates; `/agr add` works the same way.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-17)

- `/agr` now lists the commands after the status, and `/agr help` shows them on their own.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-17)

- Per-spec Pawn scale for AutoGear (`/agr scale`), with Use Pawn turned on once at first login.
- AutoGear upgrades are written into the ItemRack sets that held the replaced item: main sets of the active spec, or the sets you list.
- AutoGear pauses while a situational ItemRack set is worn and during spec switches.
- Works with ItemRack Universal and ItemRack (Forever).
